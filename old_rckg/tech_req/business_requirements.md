# Business Requirements Document (BRD)
**Project:** Risk Control Knowledge Graph (RCKG)
**Sponsor:** Compliance Department
**Date:** 2025-12-21

## 1. Executive Summary
The organization currently relies on disconnected spreadsheets for compliance, creating "Logic Silos." **RCKG** introduces an autonomous intelligence engine designed to ingest, de-conflict, and synthesize disparate industry risk and control catalogs into a unified, canonical **Logic Fabric**.

Its purpose is to provide a single, high-integrity source of truth for technology risk, reducing redundant work and accelerating new market entry. The system transforms the compliance function from a manual clerical task to a high-speed intelligence operation.

## 2. Personas

### Primary Persona: The Knowledge Engineer
The "curator of the map." Their goal is to maintain the accuracy, connectivity, and lean state of the graph.
*   **Goal:** Logical Precision.
*   **Responsibilities:** Managing data ingestion, resolving semantic conflicts, and overseeing the autonomous "Grooming" of the graph.
*   **Success Metric:** Ratio of unique, high-quality "Canonical Nodes" to redundant "Source Nodes."

## 3. Functional Requirements: The Four-Stage Knowledge Pipeline

### Stage 1: Multi-Format Ingestion ("The Harvest")
*   **Requirement:** Ingest structured (NIST OSCAL), semi-structured (Excel/CSV), and unstructured (PDF/Text) data.
*   **Action:** Raw data is ingested into a **PostgreSQL Staging Layer** where entities are flattened.
*   **Integrity Gate:** Hash-based deduplication to prevent re-processing identical files.

### Stage 2: Entity Resolution & De-confliction ("The Refinery")
*   **Syntactic Check:** Identify existing entities by unique ID (e.g., "NIST AC-2").
*   **Semantic Check:** Use `pgvector` to identify "Semantic Near-Duplicates" (intent-based matching).
*   **Constraint:** Nodes with $>0.92$ similarity to existing nodes must be flagged for the **Conflict Resolution Queue**.
*   **Conflict Logic:** Detect contradictory requirements (e.g., conflicting retention periods).

### Stage 3: Graph Synthesis & Enrichment ("The Logic Fabric")
*   **Canonical Mapping:** "Merge" multiple source controls into a single **Canonical Control Node** in **Memgraph**.
*   **Automated Link Prediction:** **Grooming Agent** suggests relationships between Risks (e.g., MITRE ATLAS) and Controls.
*   **Facet Tagging:** Automatically classify nodes by Asset Type, Function, Impact, and Frequency.

### Stage 4: Pipeline Maintenance ("The Grooming")
*   **Orphan Detection:** Identify nodes with zero relationships.
*   **Path Optimization:** Identify redundant edges to ensure shortest reasoning path ($<3$ hops).
*   **Lineage Preservation:** Maintain link back to PostgreSQL Source-of-Truth.

## 4. Technical Requirements & Integrity Guardrails
*   **Storage Separation:**
    *   **PostgreSQL**: Immutable record storage and vector embeddings.
    *   **Memgraph**: In-memory relational reasoning.
*   **Logic Consistency:** Bi-directional sync between UI edits and database components.
*   **Auditability:** Every "Merge," "Prune," or "Link" action is recorded in an **Audit Log** with timestamp and actor.
*   **Explainability:** AI-suggested links must include a **Reasoning Trace** (generated by Judge Layer/DeepSeek-R1).

## 5. Success Metrics (KPIs)

| Metric | Target | Business Impact |

| **Deduplication Rate** | **> 90%** | **Efficiency**: Eliminate redundant requirements. |
| **Logic Path Accuracy** | **> 95%** | **Trust**: High expert agreement with AI suggestions. |

## 6. Operational "HITL" Protocol
The pipeline is never fully autonomous. The Knowledge Engineer has a **Resolution Interface** to:
1.  **Merge**: Combine semantic duplicates.
2.  **Override**: Force/Delete links that AI missed or hallucinated.
3.  **Branch**: Explicitly separate similar-looking controls with distinct technical parameters.

## 7. Integration & Access Channels

### 7.1 Enterprise Integration (API-First)
*   **Requirement**: Seamless bidirectional integration with existing Enterprise GRC platforms (ServiceNow, RSA Archer).
*   **Function**: RCKG acts as the "Intelligence Engine," pushing curated Canonical Nodes and Links to the enterprise record systems via generic REST APIs or Webhooks.
*   **Use Case**: A user in ServiceNow requests a control mapping; RCKG computes it and returns the result.

### 7.2 Native User Interface (Stateless UI)
*   **Requirement**: A lightweight, standalone frontend for users who do not have access to an enterprise GRC platform (e.g., SMEs, Auditors, or smaller subsidiaries).
*   **Nature**: **Stateless**. The UI retains no local data; it purely visualizes and manipulates the backend Graph/Postgres state.
*   **Key Views**: Ingestion Wizard, Graph Explorer, and Resolution Interface.
